Curriculum

An eight-week path from screen-watcher to systematic trader.

Every module pairs recorded theory with live market reps. You graduate with a written plan, backtested setups, and a sizing model you actually trust.

Market structure & liquidity

Read the auction. Understand where stops sit, where value builds, and how to position ahead of the crowd instead of behind it.

Playbook construction

Build three high-conviction setups — opening drive, failed breakout, and afternoon rotation — each with defined invalidation.

Risk & position sizing

Fixed fractional sizing, daily loss caps, and drawdown math that keeps you in the game long enough for edge to compound.

Backtesting & stats

Turn intuition into data. Sample size, expectancy, MAE/MFE, and the numbers that actually determine whether you have an edge.

Live market reps

Daily pre-market prep, live open commentary, and post-session review. You watch the system run in real conditions, not a slideshow.

Psychology & process

Trade journals, tilt protocols, and the routines that separate traders who last from traders who blow up in month three.
